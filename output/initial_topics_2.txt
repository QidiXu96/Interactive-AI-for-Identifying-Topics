Identify topic: Impact of the pandemic on service delivery (referral practices, operational changes)  
Clues:  
- “We don’t do it here even if it is possible because the structure is small.”  
- “We stop all incoming patients at the steps and ask them to put on their face masks.”  
- “We put a hold on deliveries because of the night curfew. Though we don’t run night shift, we write our phone numbers on the pregnant mothers clinic cards to call anyone of us in a case they go into labor at night.”  
- “The only thing we changed at the height of the Corona. We used to tell any patient that goes to our home for medical treatment, to meet us at the facility.”

Reasoning:  
The respondent describes operational adjustments like mask mandates, halted home visits, and curfew-related delivery protocols. They also note that while patient inflow initially dropped due to fear, services eventually resumed. These changes directly relate to how the facility adapted its operations and service delivery during the pandemic.
